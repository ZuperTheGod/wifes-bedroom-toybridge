Toy Bridge Launcher is installed.

One more thing before it's useful: it doesn't come with any game. Open the
Launcher, click "Browse..." next to "Game:" and point it at your own
already-installed copy of a compatible game (Wife's Bedroom, ModRoom, or a
similar mod of it).

If that game hasn't been set up for toy support yet, click "Patch Game..."
right after - it's a one-time, one-click step (it also backs up the
original file automatically first).

Then: start Intiface Central and connect your toy, pick an intensity
profile in the Launcher, and click Start.
